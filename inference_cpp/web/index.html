shop-recommender Source on GitHub

Don't know what to buy next? Let me recommend something for you.

Enter your shopper ID and your shelf fills with the products you're most likely to want, picked from 235,061 items in the RetailRocket store.

  • You've shopped here before: products matched to your own views, carts and purchases by a Two-Tower model.
  • You're new here: we don't know your taste yet, so you'll see the store's best sellers.
Or try as:

  1. Enter your shopper ID to fill your shelf.

Product IDs are anonymized in the RetailRocket dataset, so each tag shows an ID and a barcode drawn from it rather than a product name.

Hosted on a free plan: if nobody has visited for 15 minutes, the first request takes about a minute while the server wakes up.

API: /health, /recommendations
